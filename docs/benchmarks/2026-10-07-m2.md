## Findings

For DN-M2-001's open questions and the follow-up tuning (DN-M2-003):

- **Positions (location integrity).** 11 of Dublin's 27 rows and 7 of Galway's 27 show 0.0 km (Greystones: 1 of 29).
  One of them is known to be about 13 km away and a café is named for another district, so HERE appears to place
  some places at the city's centre point. One Galway Scenic result is about 60 km away but shown at 0.9 km. For
  these places distance, ranking and Navigate are all wrong.
  DN-M2-003 investigation (2026-10-07): most 0.0 km rows are real places on the test point's street; see
  docs/benchmarks/2026-10-10-m2-rerun.md.
- **Nearness against category.** Both Family failures put a nearby non-family business at #1 (a salon at 0.0 km, an
  escape-room venue at 0.4 km). Whether HERE files them under a Family code or nearness lifted a secondary match
  isn't visible on screen; DN-M2-003 checks the score inputs. In Greystones Explore an open place 3.8 km away
  outranks one 0.3 km away. The weights fit the open-now bonus (5) outweighing about 3.5 km of nearness (about 4.7),
  but a weaker category match for the nearer place would also explain it.
- **Family's ceiling.** Family showed 4, 5 and 4 rows. HERE's Family search maps to two kinds, so with the cap at
  most four rows have a Family kind as their primary kind; a fifth has another. In Galway the two clear family places
  are 10–12 km away.
- **Per-kind cap.** In Dublin Outdoors the cap of two parks drops further parks and admits uncertain rows. Dublin
  Explore shows three rows and no museums, likely because the search takes HERE's 20 nearest results and the cap
  keeps two per kind; museums closed at 21:00 would also be dropped.
- **Rejected rows.** A salon, an escape-room venue and a promenade in Family, and a beach in Explore.
- **Still unclear.** Tour, boat-trip and bike-hire businesses in Explore, and a Scenic row whose evening hours
  suggest a restaurant.
- **Language.** One Galway place's details showed its opening hours in Irish.
- **Sampling.** The evening run drops closed venues in Coffee, Food, Family and Explore; a daytime re-run may differ.
