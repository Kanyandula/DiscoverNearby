## Investigation (DN-M2-003)

The causes found on 2026-10-07, before the changes:
- **Amusement code on non-family businesses.** HERE files a salon, a likely gaming arcade, escape rooms and other
  businesses with its Amusement Park code as their primary category, so they scored a full Family match and won on
  nearness.
- **One kind for all of Family.** All of HERE's Family codes mapped to one kind, so the cap of two let the two nearest
  amusement-coded places take both slots.
- **The missing Aquarium code.** HERE's Aquarium code was neither searched nor mapped.
- **Positions.** Most 0.0 km rows are real places on the test point's street. A few Dublin records share one
  street-level point without a house number. The mispositioned Scenic record agrees with its own address and access
  point. No field separates the bad positions from real ones (ADR-001 Known limitations).
