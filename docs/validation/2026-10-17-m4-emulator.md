## Defects

Ticketed in the vault for Product to rank. Rotary changes wait for a Product decision (ADR-002).

- **D1 — Rotary can't reach rows above the screen (F), DN-UX-009.** Turning up through a list scrolled down skips
  the rows above the visible area and jumps from the first visible row to the Back arrow; turning down from Back
  then starts at the first visible row, so those rows stay unreachable by rotary. Likely cause: DN-UX-006's
  `revealNextOnFocus` brings the next row into view only downward, and the rotary service moves only to items on
  screen. E1 found every row reachable before DN-UX-005's taller rows, so the taller rows probably exposed it.
  Expected: every row reachable in both directions (this ticket; docs/02 §16).
- **D2 — A quick turn skips rows going down (F), DN-UX-010.** A turn 0.22 s after the partly visible row 3 took
  focus went to the rail, skipping rows 4 and 5; with about 3 s between turns every row was reached. Likely cause:
  the reveal scroll hadn't landed when the next turn was processed.
- **Status:** both fixed on `dn-ux-009-rotary-list-reach` (Product Lead's decision, 2026-10-17), and F's re-run
  passed (below).

## F re-run after DN-UX-009 and DN-UX-010

The Product Lead chose to fix both defects (2026-10-17, ADR-002). Cause: the rotary service moves only to rows on
screen, and Compose leaves rows off screen out of its tree, so at a list's visible edge the next item in order was
outside the list. The fix (`ui/RotaryScroll.kt` and `RecommendationRow`, branch `dn-ux-009-rotary-list-reach`):

- A focused row brings the edges of the rows above and below it into view, so the next turn either way finds a row
  on screen.
- Both lists are marked as rotary scroll containers. When a quick turn reaches the list's visible edge first, the
  service keeps focus and sends a rotary scroll, and the focused row brings its neighbours into view again.
- Only the rotary service moves focus (docs/03 §17).

### Hand-driven run on the first fix (replaced)

The first fix moved focus in the app on the service's scroll (Tab or Shift+Tab, `ce34a8e`), and its scripted check
passed. The Product Lead's hand-driven run (parked, live data, Outdoors at location B, 5 rows) found:

- **Down:** Back, rows 1–5, then the rail, one move per click (3 runs).
- **Up:** rows above the screen were reached, but each one reached through the list scroll was followed by a click
  that moved nothing.
- **A fast spin up** (about 200 clicks in 5 s) sent focus past Back and round the rail while the service still held
  row 2. It ended on Back once the spin stopped.
- **Cause:** Compose sends a focus event only for nodes already in its previous accessibility snapshot. The service
  never learned of the app's move to a row that had been off screen, so it turned on from the row it knew. The scripted
  check had the same dead click after row 1 going up, hidden by its spare turns.

The Product Lead stopped the run to fix it first; the fix above replaced it.
