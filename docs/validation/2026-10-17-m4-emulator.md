# M4 automotive validation on the emulator — 2026-10-17

Ticket DN-M4-001. The docs/04 scenarios A–T that apply to the core scope, run on the reference emulator after M0
(waived V7), M1, M2 and M3. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-17-dn-m4-001-automotive-validation.md`. Live HERE
data appears in the run's screenshots, so those stay out of the repo; this record holds outcomes and name-free notes.

## Setup

- **Emulator:** `AAOS_AOSP_33_userdebug`, cold-booted without the snapshot (`-no-snapshot-load`). Image
  `system-images;android-33;android-automotive;x86_64` revision 5, `userdebug`, build `TEA1.250515.001`. Emulator
  36.4.9.0. Play services present; no Play Store or Maps (docs/04 §2).
- **Toolchain:** Android Studio 2026.2 (AI-262.9437.185.2621.16467767); Compose BOM `2026.09.00`.
- **Apps:** Discover Nearby debug build and the stub navigation app, installed from `dn-m4-001-automotive-validation`
  (`main` at `b88d27d` plus docs), user 10. The only `geo:` handler is the stub.
- **Distraction optimisation:** `get-do-activities` lists `MainActivity`.
- **UX restrictions while moving:** requires DO, restrictions `0xff`, max cumulative content items **21**, max content
  depth 3. With the limit at 21, lists show at most 5 rows in Drive.
- **Data:** live HERE (key set) unless a row names a fake scenario (`--es scenario <FakeScenario>`); Tripadvisor
  photos and ratings on live rows.
- **Input:** engineering drives touch, Park/Drive, permissions and failures over adb (`input tap`, `car_service`
  VHAL injection, `pm`, `emu geo fix`). The rotary journey is hand-driven in Extended Controls → Car rotary.

## Defects

Ticketed in the vault for Product to rank. Rotary changes wait for a Product decision (ADR-002).

- **D1 — Rotary can't reach rows above the screen (F).** Turning up through a list scrolled down skips the rows above
  the visible area and jumps from the first visible row to the Back arrow; turning down from Back then starts at the
  first visible row, so those rows stay unreachable by rotary. DN-UX-006's `revealNextOnFocus` brings the next row
  into view only downward, and the rotary service moves only to items on screen. Expected: every row reachable in
  both directions (docs/02 §16, the V7 gate).
- **D2 — Fast turns skip rows going down (F).** With turns about 0.2 s apart, focus jumps from the partly visible
  row 3 to the rail, skipping rows 4 and 5; at about 3 s per turn every row is reached. The reveal scroll hasn't
  landed when the next turn is processed.
